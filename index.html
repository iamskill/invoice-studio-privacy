Invoice Studio Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

Invoice Studio is a free, ad-supported app from Decor Designs for creating invoices and estimates, recording payments and expenses, and exporting PDFs. This policy explains how information is handled.

Your business records

Business profile details, client details, products and services, invoices, estimates, expenses, payment records, recurring schedules and your selected logo are stored locally in the app on your device. We do not operate a user-account, cloud-sync, payment-processing or invoice-delivery server. Recording a payment does not move money.

Android may back up and restore app data according to your device and Google account settings. Those system services are controlled by Android and your chosen backup provider. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may remove local records. Keep copies of important PDFs.

Optional permissions

  • Contacts: when you choose to import a client, the app can read the selected contact's name, phone and email. Import is optional; you can enter clients manually. Your address book is not uploaded to us or used for advertising.
  • Photos/files: Android's picker lets you choose a business logo. The app stores a resized copy locally. It does not request unrestricted access to your photo library.
  • Notifications: optional local reminders help you review overdue invoices. Background restrictions may delay reminders or recurring draft creation. Notifications do not send invoices to clients.

PDF sharing

PDFs are generated on your device and may include the business and client details, line items, tax, payment and other information you entered. They leave the app only when you choose a destination through Android's sharing menu. The receiving app or recipient handles that copy under its own policies. Temporary PDF copies in the app cache are eligible for cleanup after seven days when a new PDF is generated.

Advertising and consent

Invoice Studio uses Google AdMob to display banner ads and occasional interstitial ads at natural navigation breaks. Google and its advertising partners may collect and share approximate location inferred from IP address, device or other identifiers (including the advertising ID), app and ad interactions, and diagnostics such as performance and crash information. These data support advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Business records and imported contacts are not provided by Invoice Studio to AdMob.

The app requests Google restricted data processing by default for ad requests. We use Google's User Messaging Platform to request applicable consent choices before requesting ads. Where required, you can revisit available choices in More → Privacy & help → Manage ad privacy choices. Availability depends on region and Google's consent requirements. Android settings also provide controls for your advertising ID. If ads cannot load, the app's core features remain available.

Learn more from Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising. AdMob data is transmitted using encryption in transit. We do not use a separate analytics account or SDK to collect your invoice records.

Your choices and retention

You can edit or delete records within the app, remove your logo, revoke contact or notification permissions in Android settings, and clear app storage or uninstall. Removing a record does not delete copies you shared elsewhere or backups controlled by your device provider. We have no server-side account or invoice database to delete. For advertising data controls, use the Google links and device settings above.

Support

If you email us, we receive the information you voluntarily send and use it to respond. Avoid sending sensitive client or payment information unless necessary. Contact: info@interiordecordesigns.in. Website: Interior Decor Designs.

Audience and changes

Invoice Studio is intended for adults managing business records and is not directed to children. We may update this policy as the app changes, with the effective date shown above.
